Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

MovAi is a song creation and sharing service. This policy explains what information we collect when you use MovAi, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Gmail address, name, and profile picture from Google. When you sign in with Gmail and a password, we store your Gmail address and a hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Your creations. Song titles, lyrics, styles, and the songs, images, and videos you create, plus the songs you list or buy in the marketplace.
  • Credits and wallet. Your song and media credit balances, packages you buy, and payment receipts you upload for admin approval.
  • Device and security information. Device model and identifier, IP address, and sign-in activity, used to protect accounts against abuse and unauthorised access.

How we use it

We use this information to provide the service, to manage your credits and purchases, to show your songs and profile to other users where you choose to publish them, and to keep accounts secure. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Sharing

Your information is processed by the service providers that power song, image, and video generation, and by the admin when reviewing payment receipts. Public songs you publish are visible to other users. Nothing else is shared with third parties.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from your Profile page. To delete your account and your data, contact us at hoeunsok138@gmail.com and we will remove your account.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hoeunsok138@gmail.com.
